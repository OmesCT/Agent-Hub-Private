2026-10-07: the user asked "which issue do I do and which does my friend do" so they could send it to a friend. I produced a 17 KB Thai markdown, two CSVs and an HTML page (and was about to publish it as an artifact) and the user stopped me: "เดี๋ยวให้แค่แบบว่าแบ่งงาน ทำไมไปถึงขั้นนั้นอะ" (just give the split, why go that far).

**Why:** they wanted a quick list to paste to a person; extra files cost time and were not asked for.

**How to apply:** for "split the work", "who does what" or "a message I can send", answer in chat with the short list (issue numbers per person, one line for any caveat that changes what the reader does). Keep the long plan, tables, rules, CSVs and any artifact as a one-line offer, never the default. Related: [[project-work-split-2026-10-07]].
